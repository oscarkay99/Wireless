Terms of Service

Last updated: June 2026

Please read these Terms of Service carefully before using the Wireless Device Repair website or booking our repair services. By accessing our site or submitting a booking, you agree to be bound by these terms.

1. Services

Wireless Device Repair provides hardware repair services for consumer electronics including iPhones, MacBooks, iPads, Apple Watches, AirPods, and Samsung devices. All repairs are carried out by certified technicians at our East Legon, Accra workshop.

We reserve the right to decline any repair if, upon inspection, we determine the device is beyond economical repair or poses a safety risk. In such cases, you will be notified and any diagnostic fee may apply.

2. Bookings and Appointments

Online bookings are subject to availability. Submitting a booking form does not guarantee a confirmed slot — we will confirm via SMS or email within 15 minutes during business hours.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please contact us at least 2 hours before your appointment. No-shows or late cancellations may result in the diagnostic fee being retained.

3. Diagnostic Fee

A ₵200 diagnostic fee applies to all devices brought in for assessment. This fee covers the technician's time to inspect and diagnose the fault and provide a transparent repair quote.

If you choose to proceed with the repair, the ₵200 diagnostic fee is credited toward the total repair cost. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic fee is non-refundable.

4. Pricing and Payment

All prices quoted are in Ghana Cedis (₵) and are inclusive of parts and labour unless otherwise stated. Quotes are valid for 7 days from the date of issue.

Payment is due upon collection of your repaired device. We accept cash and mobile money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money). We do not offer credit or deferred payment at this time.

5. Warranty

All repairs carried out by Wireless come with a lifetime warranty on parts and labour for the specific fault repaired. This warranty is voided if:

  • The device sustains physical damage (drops, liquid) after the repair.
  • The device is opened or tampered with by a third party after our repair.
  • The fault reported is unrelated to the original repair.

To make a warranty claim, return your device to our workshop with your original receipt. We will inspect and, if the claim is valid, repair it at no charge.

6. Data and Device Security

We strongly recommend backing up your device before bringing it in for repair. Wireless is not responsible for any loss of data, photos, contacts, or settings that may occur during the repair process.

Our technicians will not access your personal data, messages, or files beyond what is necessary to test the repair. We treat all customer devices with strict confidentiality.

7. Uncollected Devices

Repaired devices must be collected within 30 days of completion. Devices not collected within 90 days, after reasonable attempts to contact the owner, may be disposed of or recycled. Wireless accepts no liability for devices uncollected beyond this period.

8. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Wireless's liability in connection with any repair shall not exceed the amount paid for that repair. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, loss of data, or loss of income arising from any repair or delay in repair.
