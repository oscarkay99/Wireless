Our Story

Built out of frustration.
Grown on trust.

In 2018, our founder Marcus Chen cracked his iPhone screen and was quoted $380, nearly the cost of a new phone, with no explanation, no timeline, and zero transparency. He spent two weeks reverse-engineering the repair process and decided someone had to do it better.

Wireless opened its first workshop in East Legon, Accra with one repair station, a refurbished ultrasonic cleaner, and a promise: every customer would understand exactly what was wrong, how long it would take, and what it would cost, before we touched a single component.

Eight years later, we've completed over 48,000 repairs across six locations, earned a 4.9-star average across 12,000+ reviews, and built a team of 24 certified technicians who share the same obsession with getting repairs right.

Why Us

What Makes Wireless
Different

We didn't set out to be just another repair shop. We built Wireless around six commitments that we hold ourselves to on every single job.

Radical Transparency

Free 47-point diagnostic before any work starts. We explain exactly what's broken, walk you through the repair options, and give you a fixed quote with no surprise fees, ever.

Lifetime Warranty

Every repair is backed by our lifetime warranty on parts and labor. If the same issue returns due to our work, we fix it for free, no questions asked, no fine print.

Same-Day Service

90% of repairs leave the same day. We stock parts for all current iPhone, Samsung, and MacBook models so we're never waiting on a supplier when your device needs to be back in your hands.

Certified Technicians

Our technicians hold Apple, Samsung, and industry certifications. Every new hire completes 120 hours of internal training before they touch a customer device, no exceptions.

OEM-Grade Parts

We source components directly from certified suppliers that meet or exceed original manufacturer specs. Every screen, battery, and camera module is tested at incoming inspection, not after installation.

Live Repair Tracking

Photo updates sent at every stage via SMS or email. You'll see your device go from check-in to diagnostic to repair to quality check, never left wondering what's happening to your phone.
