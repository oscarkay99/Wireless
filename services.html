FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a repair take?

Most screen replacements take 30–60 minutes. Battery swaps are under an hour. Complex repairs like logic board work or water damage typically take 1–3 business days.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use OEM-grade components sourced from certified suppliers that meet or exceed original manufacturer specifications. Every part is tested before installation.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

The warranty covers parts and labor on the specific repair performed. If the same issue recurs due to our workmanship, we fix it completely free of charge.

Can I track my repair status?

Yes, we send photo updates at each stage via SMS or email. You'll know exactly what's happening with your device at every step from intake to completion.
